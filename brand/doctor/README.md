# Source photos — Dr. Youssef Nasser

Drop his photos in this folder (any filename, JPG or PNG). They are the source
for the site imagery and are never bundled into the build.

Best results come from 4–6 photos:

- different angles: straight on, three-quarter left, three-quarter right
- different framing: head-and-shoulders plus half-body
- different expressions: neutral and smiling
- face fully visible — no mask, no sunglasses, nothing covering the jawline
- reasonably sharp and well lit; phone photos are fine

One photo also works, but the likeness drifts more between shots.
